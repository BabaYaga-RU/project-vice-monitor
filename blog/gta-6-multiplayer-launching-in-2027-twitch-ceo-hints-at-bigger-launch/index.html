Twitch CEO's Hint

Twitch CEO Dan Clancy has indicated that he expects GTA 6's online multiplayer to launch in 2027. Speaking with Bloomberg (via IGN), Clancy said, "You are going to see this big bump with GTA 6, but then an even bigger one next year when they launch multiplayer." He noted that Twitch had spent time with Rockstar discussing plans for creator engagement. This isn't an official Rockstar confirmation, but Clancy's statement adds to existing evidence that a separate online mode is coming.

What Remains Unconfirmed

Rockstar has not commented on the multiplayer release window, nor on the PC port's timing. The only official hints have come from court documents filed in an ongoing legal battle, which suggest a standalone online component. Until Rockstar speaks, all details remain speculative. As Rock Paper Shotgun notes, there seems no obvious reason for Clancy to mislead, but the exact scope and date of GTA 6's online mode are still unverified.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
