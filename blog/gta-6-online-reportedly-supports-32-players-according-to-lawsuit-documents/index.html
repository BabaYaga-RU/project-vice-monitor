The Report

Korean gaming outlet 게임메카 has reported that GTA 6 Online will support up to 32 players, citing documents from a lawsuit as the source of the detail. The report presents the information as confirmed by those legal documents, but Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive have not publicly commented on the claim.

What This Means

If accurate, 32-player lobbies would be a significant step up from GTA Online's current 30-player cap on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, potentially enabling larger missions, races, and free-roam sessions in the next installment.

However, the report does not specify which lawsuit is referenced, nor does it provide further technical details such as whether 32 players will apply across all modes or only to certain activities.

What We Don't Know

As with any unverified legal document leak, readers should treat this as a report rather than official confirmation. No comment has been made by Rockstar, Take-Two, or the GTA 6 development team, and the exact scope of the cited documents remains unclear.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
