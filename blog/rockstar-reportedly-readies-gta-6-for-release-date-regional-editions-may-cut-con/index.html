The Report

According to Notebookcheck, Rockstar is readying GTA 6 to ship by its release date. The report also says the company is adding regional editions of the game, and that these editions may have content cut.

What Remains Unknown

Notebookcheck's report does not specify which regions would receive different editions, what content might be cut, or how the regional versions would differ from the standard release.

The exact release date and platform details are also not confirmed in the report. As with any unverified report, readers should treat these claims as rumors until Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive officially comment.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
