Leak Overview

According to a report from Eurogamer, a new leak appears to include nearly 200GB of Grand Theft Auto 5's source code. The leaked files reportedly reveal a trove of cut content, including a scrapped Liberty City DLC and very early assets related to GTA 6.

Liberty City DLC and GTA 6 Details

Fans combing through the data have reportedly found cut dialogue, unused assets, and a rough version of GTA 6's map. Eurogamer notes that YouTuber TJGM posted an outline of the supposed Liberty City DLC map's shoreline, showing a larger playable space than GTA 4's version. The report also mentions speculation that the Liberty City DLC might have been shelved for future use, though no evidence supports that claim.

Additionally, the leak reportedly includes materials linked to the previously rumored 'Agent Trevor' DLC, which was described by actor Steven Ogg and former Rockstar staff. Eurogamer emphasizes that these are leaked reports and unverified files, not officially confirmed details.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
