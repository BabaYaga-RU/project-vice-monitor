Rockstar’s Tight-Lipped Approach

Rockstar Games has reportedly compared the level of secrecy surrounding Grand Theft Auto 6 to the way Apple protects the iPhone or Coca-Cola guards its formula. The statement was covered by IGN, which did not provide further context or direct quotes in the initial report.

According to IGN, the comparison underscores how seriously Rockstar takes preventing leaks and keeping details of the highly anticipated title under wraps until official reveals.

What We Don’t Know

The report from IGN does not specify who at Rockstar made the comment, when it was made, or whether it was part of an interview, internal memo, or public statement. No additional details about GTA 6’s development, release window, or features were disclosed in this item.

As with many Rockstar announcements, fans are left parsing the company's words for hints, but this particular comparison appears aimed at emphasizing the studio's commitment to secrecy ahead of any official marketing push.
