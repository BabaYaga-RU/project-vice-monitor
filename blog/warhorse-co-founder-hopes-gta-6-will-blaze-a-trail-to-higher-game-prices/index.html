Warhorse co-founder comments on game costs

PC Gamer reports that Martin Klima, co-founder of Kingdom Come: Deliverance developer Warhorse Studios, hopes Grand Theft Auto 6 will 'blaze a trail' to higher game prices. Klima said games keep getting more expensive to make and that 'money has to come from somewhere.'

The remarks are presented as Klima's own industry observation rather than as an official statement from Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. PC Gamer's story does not include any confirmation from either company about GTA 6 pricing.

What remains unknown

It is still unclear whether Grand Theft Auto 6 will actually launch with a higher price than typical AAA releases, or whether any other publisher will follow the trail Klima hopes GTA 6 will set. No specific price point, timing, or internal Rockstar decision has been reported.

For now, the only public detail is Klima's hopeful comment, as reported by PC Gamer. Any concrete pricing plans for GTA 6 would need to come directly from Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
