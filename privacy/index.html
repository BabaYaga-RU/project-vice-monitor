Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Macca the Gator's YouTube publishing application (the “Application”) handles information when it is used to publish videos to a YouTube channel authorized by its owner.

Google and YouTube authorization

The Application uses Google OAuth 2.0 and the YouTube Data API to authenticate the channel owner and publish videos. It requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope, which is needed to upload videos to the YouTube channel the owner authorizes. The Application does not request access to other Google account data.

Information and credentials

OAuth credentials and temporary access tokens are used only to authenticate with Google and carry out requested video publishing. They are stored as protected deployment credentials where applicable, are not included in public pages or application logs, and are not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

The Application processes video files and their associated titles, descriptions, and tags to upload them to the authorized YouTube channel. The YouTube Data API processes this information under Google's policies. Video content and publication settings are managed through YouTube and the channel owner's Google account.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell users' personal data. We do not share Google user data with third parties except as needed for the Application to communicate with Google and YouTube to provide the requested upload functionality, or where disclosure is required by law. We do not use Google user data for purposes unrelated to this feature.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the Application's access at any time from their Google Account connections page. After access is revoked, the Application can no longer use that authorization to upload videos.

Data protection and retention

Access is limited to the credentials and automation process needed to provide video publishing. OAuth credentials are retained only while needed to operate the authorized integration or until access is revoked. Temporary upload files are intended to be removed from the automation runner after processing. YouTube retains successfully uploaded videos according to the channel owner's settings and Google's policies.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The Application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing YouTube upload feature described in this policy.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Macca the Gator through the official Instagram account.
