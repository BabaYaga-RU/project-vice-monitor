What the Report Says

According to Notebookcheck, a Collector's Edition pre-order has leaked what appears to be the GTA 6 map, exposing locations in Leonida. The report describes the leaked material as part of pre-order-related content, though no official confirmation from Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive has been cited.

As with any leak, the authenticity of the map has not been verified. Notebookcheck's report is currently the sole basis for this story, and Rockstar has not publicly commented.

What Remains Unknown

It is unclear how the map was obtained, whether the Collector's Edition contents are final, or how much of the full game world is represented. Until Rockstar or Take-Two addresses the leak, fans should treat the Leonida location names and layout as unconfirmed rumors.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
