What the Report Says

A post on Reddit, aggregated by Google News, claims that Rockstar Games has instructed actors working on Grand Theft Auto 6 not to disclose their roles until the game's launch on November 19. The report does not provide specific names or evidence beyond the Reddit post itself, so this remains an unverified rumor.

Context and Uncertainty

The exact date of November 19 aligns with previous speculation about a 2026 release window for GTA 6, though Rockstar has not officially confirmed an exact launch date. The extent of the alleged restriction—whether it applies to voice actors, motion capture performers, or all talent—is unknown. No other outlets have corroborated the story as of this writing.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
