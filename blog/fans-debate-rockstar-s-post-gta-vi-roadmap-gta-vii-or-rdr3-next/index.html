What the Reddit Thread Is Asking

A Reddit thread picked up by Google News asks the community for theories on Rockstar Games' post-GTA VI roadmap. The central questions are whether Rockstar has already started pre-production on GTA VII, or whether Red Dead Redemption 3 should be the studio's next priority.

The thread is fan speculation, not a leak or an official statement from Rockstar or Take-Two.

No Official Plans Confirmed

The report contains no confirmed roadmap from Rockstar or Take-Two. No evidence is cited in the Reddit thread that GTA VII is in pre-production, and no announcement has been made about RDR3.

At this point, everything in the thread remains a theory. What Rockstar actually plans after GTA VI is unknown, and the company has not publicly commented on either project.

Why It Matters

With GTA VI still the immediate focus for Rockstar, the conversation about what comes next is natural. Fans are already looking ahead to GTA VII and RDR3, but there is no official word to confirm or deny those hopes.

Macca Blog will continue to follow Rockstar and Take-Two announcements for any real roadmap updates.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
