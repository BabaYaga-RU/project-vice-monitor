What We Know

According to a report from ixbt.games, a daughter has publicly asked Rockstar Games to allow her terminally ill father to play GTA VI before its official release. The report, published on September 21, 2026, does not include the daughter's name or details about her father's condition. It also does not clarify whether the request was made via social media, a petition, or direct contact.

Rockstar has not yet issued a response as of the report's publication. The publisher notes that the request has surfaced online, but no additional context is provided about the family's situation or the timeline of the father's illness.

Community Reaction and Open Questions

The story has sparked discussion on Reddit, where users are sharing reactions and speculating on whether Rockstar might accommodate such requests. Historically, game companies have occasionally granted early access for compassionate reasons, but there is no official precedent from Rockstar.

As of now, GTA VI remains slated for its announced release window, and Rockstar has made no public announcement about any special early access program. The ixbt.games report characterizes this as a developing story, and further details—including any official reply from Rockstar—have yet to emerge.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
