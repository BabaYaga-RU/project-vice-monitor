Report: GTA VI Album Dated November 19

According to a Reddit post aggregated by Google News, Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album is coming on November 19. The post says the release will feature 34 original tracks that capture the “electric energy” of Vice City and Leonida, from a “genre-defying artist roster.”

What’s Still Unconfirmed

The brief report does not include a full tracklist, artist names, or any official artwork. It also remains unclear whether Rockstar Games or Take-Two made the announcement themselves or whether this is a fan-created listing circulating online. Until an official source confirms the album, this should be treated as a report/rumor, not established fact.

Until Official Confirmation

If accurate, the album would arrive in November, but no additional release details have been verified. Macca Blog will update as soon as Rockstar, Take-Two, or a more detailed official announcement provides confirmation.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
