Report: GTA 6 Collaborations Teased

Notebookcheck has reported that several major artists, including Travis Scott, Future, and Keith Richards, have shared teasers hinting at collaborations with GTA 6. According to the report, these musicians appear to be involved in some capacity, likely contributing to the game's soundtrack or promotional material. The teasers were posted on social media and other public platforms, though no official statement from Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive has been made.

What We Don't Know Yet

As of now, the specifics of these collaborations remain unclear. It is not known whether the artists are contributing original tracks, remixes, or are involved in other aspects of the game. The report from Notebookcheck is based solely on the artists' own teasers, and no further details have been confirmed. Rockstar and Take-Two have remained silent, leaving fans to speculate about the nature and extent of these partnerships. This is a developing story, and more information may surface soon.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
