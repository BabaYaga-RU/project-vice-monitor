What happened

According to Dexerto, Rockstar has taken legal action after modders ported Grand Theft Auto V to the Nintendo Switch. The report says the action is directed at those responsible for the unofficial port, though no further specifics were provided in the initial coverage.

This is not the first time Rockstar has targeted modding projects, but the Switch port reportedly drew attention by bringing a game that never officially appeared on Nintendo's platform to the handheld.

What remains unknown

It is still unclear what form the legal action takes — whether it is a cease-and-desist, a DMCA takedown, or a lawsuit — and which individuals or groups are named. There is also no confirmed information about how complete or playable the modded Switch version was, or where it was distributed.

Dexerto's report is the only source cited so far, and neither Rockstar Games nor Take-Two Interactive has publicly commented on the matter.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
