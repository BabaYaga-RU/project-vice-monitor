GTA 6 Pre-Orders: Xbox Sources Say PS5 Is Dominating

Notebookcheck reports that Xbox insiders indicate PlayStation 5 pre-orders for GTA 6 are significantly outpacing Xbox versions, though official numbers remain unconfirmed.

What the Report Says

According to Notebookcheck, unnamed Xbox sources claim that PlayStation 5 pre-orders for Grand Theft Auto VI are dominating. The report suggests that the PS5 version is significantly outpacing the Xbox Series X|S version in early pre-order numbers, though no specific figures are provided.

Context and Unknowns

These are unverified claims from internal Xbox channels, not official statements from Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. The full scope remains unclear: we don't know if this is a global trend or limited to certain regions, nor how this might influence future marketing or bundle deals. Notebookcheck's report is the only source cited here.

What It Could Mean

If accurate, a PS5 lead in pre-orders could reflect the larger install base of the console and the platform's historical strength in GTA titles. It could also prompt Microsoft to adjust promotional strategies ahead of the game's launch. However, until Rockstar or Take-Two release official pre-order data, these reports should be treated as speculation.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
