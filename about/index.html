About Macca Lab

Macca Lab is an independent project that hosts experiments and editorial content.

A place for projects and ideas

The site brings different experiments and project work together under the Macca Lab name. Its public editorial section is Macca Blog, which focuses on Grand Theft Auto, Rockstar Games and related gaming stories.

Macca Blog publishes sourced news, reports and analysis. Articles link to the material they rely on and distinguish confirmed information from rumors, allegations and unresolved claims.

Start with the latest Macca Blog stories, or read the RSS feed.

Independent editorial project

Macca Lab and Macca Blog are independent projects. They are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto and related names and marks belong to their respective owners.
