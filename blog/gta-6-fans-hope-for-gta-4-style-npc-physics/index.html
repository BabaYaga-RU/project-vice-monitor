Reddit Post Highlights GTA 4's Beloved NPC Physics

A Reddit user has posted a simple wish: 'i hope gta 6 has physics that let you just bully the npcs like gta 4 did.' The post, shared on Reddit, has reignited community discussion about Grand Theft Auto IV's Euphoria engine, which gave NPCs dynamic ragdoll reactions and lifelike physics when players interacted with them. Many fans fondly recall shoving pedestrians or watching them stumble realistically.

What This Means for GTA 6

Rockstar Games has not commented on any physics details for GTA 6, and this Reddit post is purely fan speculation. The sentiment reflects a common desire among players for a return to the more reactive and unpredictable NPC interactions of GTA 4, as opposed to the toned-down physics seen in GTA 5. Whether Rockstar will heed these calls remains unknown, as the studio typically reveals gameplay features closer to launch.

The Ongoing Debate

The thread adds to a long-running comparison between GTA 4 and GTA 5 physics. While some players prefer GTA 5's smoother combat, others miss the weight and chaos of Euphoria. For now, this is merely a community wish—no official statement from Take-Two or Rockstar confirms any such feature for GTA 6.

As the game's release approaches, fans will keep speculating. This Reddit post serves as a reminder that even small community voices can highlight what players truly want from the next entry in the franchise.
