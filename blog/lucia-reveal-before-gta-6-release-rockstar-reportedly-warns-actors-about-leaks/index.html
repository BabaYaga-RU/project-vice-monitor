Lucia Reveal Before GTA 6 Release? Rockstar Reportedly Warns Actors About Leaks

A new report from Notebookcheck suggests Rockstar Games has cautioned actors about leaks, making an early reveal of the character Lucia before GTA 6's release date unlikely. Here's what we know.

Report: Rockstar Warns Actors About Leaks

According to Notebookcheck, Rockstar Games has reportedly warned actors about potential leaks related to Grand Theft Auto VI. The report suggests that this internal warning makes it unlikely that the character Lucia will be officially revealed before the game's release date. However, this is based on a single source and should be treated as a rumor rather than confirmed fact.

What Remains Unknown

Notebookcheck does not provide specific details about how the warning was communicated, which actors were involved, or what specific leaks Rockstar is concerned about. It also does not clarify whether Lucia will receive a dedicated reveal later or only appear in the final game. As of now, Rockstar and Take-Two have not issued any official statement regarding this report.

Context for GTA 6 Fans

Lucia is one of the two protagonists confirmed for GTA 6 through earlier official announcements and leaked footage. Fans have been speculating about when Rockstar might show the character again, but this report suggests that a pre-release reveal could be off the table. As always, treat unverified reports with caution until Rockstar or Take-Two confirms something officially.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
