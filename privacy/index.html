Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Macca Lab website, Macca Blog and the YouTube publishing feature operated for the Macca the Gator channel.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Website

Macca Lab is a public website and does not currently offer visitor accounts or a sign-up form. When a page is requested, the hosting and network providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser and device details, the requested URL and request time to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the service. Their handling and retention of this information are governed by their own policies and service settings.

Macca Lab does not sell personal information. We do not use a site-wide analytics or advertising profile in the current website code.

Cookies and local storage

The website does not currently set its own tracking cookies. The optional Skylet chat widget stores up to eight recent chat messages in your browser's local storage so they can remain visible when you return. This is browser storage, not a cookie. You can remove it using your browser's site-data controls; if storage is unavailable, the chat can be used for the current page view.

When you use Skylet, the message you submit is sent to the Cloudflare Worker and Cloudflare Workers AI service that provide its response. Macca Lab does not operate a separate conversation-history database for the website chat.

Advertising

Macca Blog may show partner promotions through Macca Lab's own /ads/ system. That system loads configured creative images and links; it is separate from Google AdSense and is not a Google advertising placement.

The Google AdSense script is installed for site setup, but no AdSense ad placements are configured and ads are not currently being served. If placements are activated, this policy applies to that feature. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on a user's visits to this or other websites. Google explains its advertising cookies and publisher disclosure requirements in its AdSense privacy policy guidance. Users can review or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Where required, applicable consent and privacy choices will be provided before relevant advertising is served.

Google and YouTube API integration

The Macca the Gator publishing automation uses Google OAuth 2.0 and the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the YouTube channel authorized by its owner. It requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope. It does not request permission to read the channel or other Google account data.

OAuth client credentials and the persistent refresh token are held as protected GitHub Actions secrets. A temporary access token is obtained when an automation run needs to publish. Credentials and tokens are used only to authenticate and publish. Article titles, descriptions, tags, video files and publication results are processed for that purpose. The repository stores publication identifiers and article/video URLs to prevent duplicate uploads; temporary video files are removed from the Actions runner after the job.

Google API information is not used for advertising, sold, or shared except as needed to communicate with Google and YouTube for this feature or as required by law. The integration's use and transfer of Google API data follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The channel owner can revoke access from their Google Account connections.

Third-party services and links

The site relies on Render for hosting and Cloudflare for the optional Skylet AI service. Macca Blog links to independent publishers and may display images hosted by those publishers. Social, source, advertising and API links may take you to services with their own privacy practices; review those providers' policies when you use them.

Data retention

Chat messages saved in your browser remain in local storage until you clear them or your browser removes site data. Technical hosting logs, if collected, are retained under the hosting provider's policies and configuration. The YouTube automation retains its credentials only while the integration is authorized and keeps article/video publication records to prevent duplicate posts. Temporary video files are removed when a GitHub Actions job ends.

Your choices

  • Clear Macca Lab site data in your browser to remove the locally saved Skylet conversation.
  • Choose whether to open third-party links and review their privacy settings.
  • Revoke Google authorization using your Google Account connections page.
  • If Google ads are added, use the linked Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info controls for available advertising choices.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Macca the Gator through the official Instagram account.
