What Was Reported

According to Sportskeeda, Take-Two's CEO commented on the extended look at Grand Theft Auto 6, saying it had "an element of tutorial baked in there." The remark was made in a context that appears to describe how the game's early moments might guide players through mechanics, though the exact setting or interview was not detailed in the report.

What This Means for GTA 6

The CEO's phrasing suggests that Rockstar may integrate tutorial elements directly into the game's opening sequences, rather than relying on separate loading screens or pop-up instructions. This aligns with a trend in AAA titles to teach through gameplay, but no specifics about the tutorial's structure or scope were provided. Whether this applies to the full game or just the showcased extended look remains unclear.

Uncertainties and Next Steps

As of now, this is a single-source report from Sportskeeda. We don't know when the CEO made these comments, in what forum, or if Rockstar has confirmed any tutorial-related features. Until more details emerge or official announcements are made, this should be treated as a reported statement rather than confirmed game design. Fans awaiting the next GTA 6 trailer or gameplay reveal will have to see if tutorials are a visible part of the experience.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
