Leaked Merchandise Details

According to a report from RockstarINTEL, new details about GTA 6 collectables and merchandise have surfaced online. The report claims that Rockstar Games is preparing a wide range of products as part of the game's upcoming launch, but specific items, pricing, and release dates were not disclosed.

The source, which has not been independently verified, describes the leak as evidence of Rockstar's 'massive launch' strategy, but stops short of confirming any official announcements. As with any unconfirmed leak, readers should treat this information as speculative until Rockstar or Take-Two provides official word.

What Remains Unknown

At this time, no official images, product lists, or retail partnerships have been revealed. RockstarINTEL does not specify where the leak originated or whether it includes digital or physical merchandise. The exact scope of the collectables—such as statues, apparel, or in-game bonuses—also remains unclear.

This is a developing story. We'll update this post as more credible sources emerge or if Rockstar makes an official statement. For now, treat these reports as early rumors in the long wait for GTA 6.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
