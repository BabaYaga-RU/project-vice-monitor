Alleged Collector’s Edition Images Surface

Notebookcheck is reporting that pictures purportedly showing a Grand Theft Auto 6 Collector’s Edition have leaked online. The report notes the apparent leak arrives despite there being no physical disc pre-orders for the game, raising questions about the images’ authenticity and timing.

As with any unofficial leak, the images should be treated as unverified. The report does not confirm whether the pictured items are genuine, and no official announcement from Rockstar Games has been cited.

What Remains Unknown

Details are still thin. Notebookcheck does not specify the exact contents, pricing, or release date of the alleged Collector’s Edition, nor does it explain why physical disc pre-orders are reportedly unavailable.

Until Rockstar or Take-Two confirms the edition, this is best viewed as a rumor. Readers should wait for an official reveal before drawing conclusions about GTA 6 special editions.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
