Release After Long Wait

According to Rock Paper Shotgun, Nivalis Nights has finally launched on Steam after a lengthy development cycle. The game was first revealed in 2021 with an original 2023 release window, but several delays pushed it to 2026. The publication notes that the game, formerly known simply as Nivalis, brings players back to the cyberpunk world of Cloudpunk.

Gameplay and Features

The game is described by developer ION LANDS as a cyberpunk slice-of-life sim. You start by running a ramen shop, where you can design seating, decorations, and the menu. Rock Paper Shotgun highlights the need for a varied menu, noting you can't just serve a crummy bowl of noodles. Beyond the restaurant management, players can freely explore the city, meet NPCs, pursue romance, embark on various quests, go fishing on a boat, and even grow their own produce. The publication also mentions that you can eventually manage other restaurants or nightclubs, adding to the simulation depth.

Availability

Nivalis Nights is available right now on Steam. The voxel aesthetic helps create a dense, atmospheric cityscape, and the game mixes narrative with simulation elements. While it's not a fully open world, the city is yours to explore. Rock Paper Shotgun's report confirms the release, though details on pricing and post-launch support remain limited at this time.
