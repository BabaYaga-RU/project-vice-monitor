Former GTA 6 Designer Questions Switch 2 Version, Citing ‘Subpar Experience’

A former GTA 6 designer reportedly says a Switch 2 port makes little sense due to a potentially subpar experience, suggesting fans would buy a PS5 instead.

What Was Reported

A report circulating via Reddit quotes a former GTA 6 designer arguing that a Switch 2 version of Grand Theft Auto VI makes little sense. The designer reportedly said that such a port would deliver a potentially "subpar experience" and that fans would "buy a PS5" instead.

Context and Uncertainty

The claim is based on a single Reddit report and has not been confirmed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two. It remains unclear which project the designer worked on, whether they have direct knowledge of any Switch 2 plans, or if any version for Nintendo's next console is even in development.

No official details about GTA 6 on Switch 2 have been announced, so this should be treated as an unverified report rather than established fact.

What to Watch

Until Rockstar or Take-Two addresses the possibility, a Switch 2 version of GTA 6 remains speculative. The report does add to ongoing fan debate about whether the game could run on less powerful hardware without major compromises.

Sources and notes

Claims, reports, leaks and rumors are attributed to their original sources. Unconfirmed information is labeled clearly and should not be read as established fact.
