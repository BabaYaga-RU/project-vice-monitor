What Rockstar announced

Rockstar Games has opened pre-orders for a limited ‘Goodtime State Vice City’ collection of Grand Theft Auto 6 collectibles priced at £349, according to Eurogamer. The set does not include a copy of GTA 6.

Eurogamer reports the package is available to pre-order now, though the full contents list has not been detailed in the report beyond several notable items.

The 'suspicious' items

Among the revealed items are a magnetic mirror, a razor-blade keychain, and a swizzle spoon. As Eurogamer points out, combined those three items resemble tools commonly associated with preparing certain substances. The outlet describes the inclusion as presumably intentional, given GTA’s provocative history and drug-related storylines.

Rockstar’s listing reportedly makes no such joke or acknowledgment. The mirror’s description does invite buyers to ‘Reflect on your life choices,’ which Eurogamer flags as a funny gag. A crocodile statue is also included as a desk piece.

What’s still unknown

Eurogamer’s report doesn’t confirm the full contents of the collection, shipping details, or whether Rockstar will officially acknowledge the obvious drug-paraphernalia reading. It also remains unclear if the £349 price is meant to be taken seriously or as another cheeky GTA gag.

For now, this is a single-source report from Eurogamer, and Rockstar/Take-Two haven’t commented further. We’ll update if more details surface.
